Importance of Civil Procedure Code (CPC)

The Civil Procedure Code, 1908 is the backbone of civil litigation in India. It provides the procedural framework for filing suits, conducting trials, passing decrees, execution of judgments, and appeals.

  • Ensures fair trial and due process of law
  • Defines jurisdiction of courts
  • Regulates pleadings, evidence, and execution
  • Provides remedies like appeals, review, and revision
  • Protects substantive rights through procedural safeguards

CPC Syllabus – LAW305

L-T-P-C: 4-1-0-5 | Semester: VIII

Course Objectives

  • To understand fundamentals of Civil Procedure and Powers of Court.
  • To study appeals, revision, review and limitation in civil proceedings.

UNIT I – Definitions

Affidavit, Decree, Order, Judgment, Decree Holder, Judgment Debtor, Mesne Profits.

UNIT II – Jurisdiction & Institution of Suits

Subject Matter, Pecuniary and Territorial Jurisdiction, Cognizance, Res Sub Judice, Res Judicata, Foreign Judgment, Institution of Suits, Pleadings, Plaint, Written Statement, Set-off, Counter Claim, Discovery, Trial Procedure, Court Fees & Suit Valuation.

UNIT III – Special Suits, Appeals & Review

Suits of Special Nature, Suits by/against Government, Aliens, Public Nuisance, Summary Suits, Transfer of Cases, Caveat, Appeals, Review, Revision, Reference, Inherent Powers of Court, Restitution.

UNIT IV – Execution

Meaning of Execution, Execution Applications, Arrest & Detention, Attachment, Sale of Property, Delivery of Property, Distribution of Assets.

UNIT V – Law of Limitation

Concept & Object of Limitation, Extension of Period, Legal Disability, Continuous Running of Time, Acknowledgment, Continuing Tort, Acquisition of Ownership by Possession, ADR (Summary).

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand key definitions under CPC.
  • Understand jurisdiction and institution of suits.
  • Understand appeals, review, revision and transfer of cases.
  • Understand execution of decrees.
  • Understand limitation and time-bar principles.

A strong understanding of CPC is essential for advocates, judges, judicial aspirants, and legal professionals as it governs the entire civil justice delivery system.
