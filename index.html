About CPC Course Portal

The Civil Procedure Code (CPC) Course Portal is an interactive academic platform designed to support classroom learning, continuous assessment, and structured academic monitoring.

This portal provides access to notes, assignments, quizzes, video lectures, feedback submission, and an integrated FAQ system for doubt clarification.

Why Study CPC?

The Civil Procedure Code, 1908 forms the backbone of civil litigation in India. It governs jurisdiction, pleadings, trial procedures, execution of decrees, appeals, and limitation principles.
